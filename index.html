01 / THE BOY BEHIND THE STORY

Not just a name.
A whole story.

My name is Collins Munene. Some know me as Collo, others as Guapo, and a few know me as Coleywins. But whichever name you use, you're talking about the same guy.

I was once a little boy with a mischievous mind, plenty of energy and ambitions that sometimes seemed bigger than my age.

This website is my attempt to preserve the moments, people, places and experiences that have helped shape my life.

It isn't a story about having everything figured out. It's about growing, learning, laughing, dreaming and appreciating the people who have walked alongside me.

"Forget the past, not the lessons."

— COLLINS MUNENE

YOUR EXISTING NUMBER

My Father — Papa

Everybody calls him Papa. At home, among relatives and even at his workplace, that is the name people know him by. But to Collins, he is much more than a father. He is a man whose life, determination and sacrifices have become one of the biggest sources of inspiration.

Papa knows what it means to work hard. Coming from a humble background, with limited opportunities for education, he had to find his own way through life. His journey was not handed to him. He had responsibilities to carry, obstacles to overcome and a family that depended on him. There were times when work meant barely getting enough sleep at home. There were days when his body had to do the work so that his children could have school fees, basic necessities and opportunities he did not have growing up. Collins understands that love does not always come in the form of long conversations or one-on-one moments. Sometimes it comes through the sacrifices a parent makes every single day.

As Collins once put it:

"We might have not experienced one on one love him alot but we know he work off his body so that we can grow up and have a better life than him."
030

The Cool Kid with a Camera

Before he became the man we know as Papa, there was a young man with his own style, a camera, and a whole lot of ambition. Just look at that outfit — cap tilted sideways, baggy jeans, and safari boots. He clearly had his own fashion agenda!

After high school, Papa ventured into photography and worked as a cameraman. He took on different gigs, capturing moments and putting his skills to work wherever opportunities arose. Every assignment was another step towards the future he was determined to create.

Those gigs were more than just jobs. They were part of the journey — the effort, the hustle, and the sacrifices that gradually helped him get to where he is today. Long before the vehicles, the responsibilities, and the life he built, there was this young man figuring things out and refusing to sit around waiting for opportunities.

Sometimes I look at this picture and realise that Papa's story did not begin with everything he has today. It began with a young man willing to work for it. The style was already there, but so was the determination.

"Before the man who built so much, there was a young man who dared to start somewhere."

030

Ntaû — The Man Who Planted a Dream

His name was Munoru, but between us, he was Ntaû. A name that carries memories, affection and a connection that I will always treasure. He was more than a grandfather to me. He was someone whose influence would eventually shape one of my biggest ambitions in life.

I remember being in Class Six when Ntaû gave me a female goat. He did not just give me an animal and leave me to figure things out. He taught me how to feed it and take care of it. What started as one goat gradually became a little farming experience of my own.

The goat eventually gave birth to a male kid, and later on, twins. Before I knew it, I had a small collection of goats. Eventually, I sold them, but I never sold the memories or the lessons that came with them.

Looking back, I realise that Ntaû had planted something much bigger than a farming interest in me. He had introduced me to a world that I would eventually dream of making my own.

Today, I dream of becoming a very big farmer. I want to build something substantial in agriculture, including pursuing my interest in broiler farming. Whenever I think about where that dream began, I remember the goat Ntaû gave me and the lessons he taught me.

A Goodbye I Will Never Forget

There is another memory that will always remain with me. One Thursday, my parents picked me up from school. It was not an ordinary pickup. They had come to tell me that Ntaû had passed away.

I felt terrible. His funeral became the first time I can remember crying. Losing someone who had played such an important role in my life was something I could never simply brush aside.

Since he died, I have never forgotten him. I still carry the memories, the lessons and the dreams that began with him.

Perhaps one day, when I have built the large farms I dream about, I will look back and remember that it all began with one female goat and a grandfather who believed in teaching me something valuable.

Some people leave you memories. Others leave you dreams that you spend the rest of your life trying to fulfil.

Ntaû, I will never forget you.
